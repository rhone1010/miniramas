Portraits of adults only, for now

Liten & Co currently creates portraits of adults 18 and over.

We’re taking a little more time to understand the evolving policies and usage rights surrounding the transformation of images and likenesses of people under 18, both in the U.S. and internationally.

For now, photos of anyone we believe to be under 18 will be declined before anything is crafted or charged.

We hope to offer this in future as the policies and protections around younger subjects become clearer. We’ll keep you posted.

A note about your photos

Your photograph is used only to create your Liten & Co pieces. We don’t sell them, use them to train AI models, or share them with others.
