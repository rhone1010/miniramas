Ten questions

✦ What happens to my photograph?

We use it to craft your portrait, and then we delete it. We do not train anything on it, we do not sell it, and we do not show it to anyone.

The one exception is a dispute. If you raise a complaint we need your photograph to look into what happened, so we hold it until that is settled — and we will tell you if yours is one we are keeping. The privacy policy says it in full.

✦ Will it actually look like the person?

That is the whole job. Every finish is built to hold the face first and the material second, and each craft is checked against your photograph before it reaches you.

It is not a filter over your picture — it is a new piece made from it, so it will not be identical. The likeness should be unmistakable. See for yourself in the gallery: every finish we make, on two sitters.

✦ What if I don't like what I get?

We craft it again, or we give your credits back, or we refund you. In that order, and the first one is usually the right one.

Nobody has to argue their case. If a portrait did not work for you, that is enough.

✦ How does it work?

Upload a photograph, choose a finish, and we craft a portrait from it. It usually takes under a minute.

There are fifty-six finishes in eight rooms — stone, glass, ink, handmade things, other centuries. The Curator will help you choose once you are inside.

✦ Why was my photograph turned away?

Six things stop a photograph outright: no face in it, more than one person, the face too small in the frame, heavy blur, the face mostly hidden, or so much light or shadow that the features are gone.

Everything else we will attempt. If we flagged something and let you through anyway, that is us saying this may come out softer than you would like — not this will fail.

✦ Can I use a photograph of my child?

A family photograph with children in it, yes. A photograph of a child on their own, no — we will refuse it.

✦ What does it cost?

Crafting is paid for in credits, bought in advance and spent one portrait at a time. Prices are shown before you pay, and credits do not expire while your account is open.

Prints are priced separately, by size and framing.

✦ Can I print it, and is it mine?

Yes to both. Every piece in your collection can be sent to print, framed or unframed, and it goes to a professional print house rather than an office printer. We upscale the piece first, so something that looks right on screen holds up on a wall.

The crafted image is yours. Print it, hang it, give it away, use it commercially if you like.

✦ How long until my print arrives?

You will get a note with tracking on the day it leaves the press. We would rather send you that than a guess.

If a print arrives damaged or misprinted, tell us and we will sort it out.

✦ Who are you?

Liten & Co is a small studio in California. We built this because the pictures worth keeping are mostly sitting on a phone, and a portrait of someone you love ought to be an object.

We are in soft launch, which means a small number of people are using it while we get it right. If something is wrong, we want to hear it.

Terms of Use

Who we are

What the service does

You upload a photograph. We use it to craft a new image — a stylised portrait in one of our finishes. You can keep that image and order it as a print. The crafted image is generated by software: a new work made from your photograph, not a filter applied to it, and it will not be identical to what you supplied.

Your account

You need an account to buy anything, and you must be 18 or over to hold one. Keep your sign-in details to yourself — anything done through your account is treated as done by you.

The photographs you upload

You must have the right to upload the photograph. By uploading it you confirm that you took it or have permission to use it, and that everyone shown in it has agreed to it being used this way.

A family photograph that includes children is fine. A photograph of a child on their own is not, and we will refuse it.

You must not upload photographs of people who have not agreed to it, images you do not have the right to use, sexual content, or anything unlawful. We can refuse or remove anything, and close an account that does this.

What you are granting us

A licence to craft your image, and nothing else.

You keep ownership of your photograph. You grant us permission to store and process it for the sole purpose of crafting the images you ask for, and fulfilling any print you order.

We do not use your photographs to train anything. We do not sell them, share them, license them onward, or use them to advertise Liten & Co. If we ever want to show a piece publicly we will ask first, and you can say no. This licence ends when we delete the photograph.

How long we keep your photograph

We delete your source photograph once the work using it is finished. We hold it only while it is needed — during a craft, and through a commission until the piece is accepted.

If a dispute is open we keep it until that is closed, because we cannot look into a complaint without it. A dispute may stay open for up to a year, and we will tell you if yours is one that is keeping your photograph with us. We would rather settle it quickly, and will do what we can to reach you and put it right.

Your crafted images stay in your collection until you remove them or close your account.

What you own

The crafted image is yours. Print it, hang it, give it away, use it commercially. We keep our own materials — the finishes themselves, the software, and everything on this site that we made.

Credits and payment

Crafting is paid for in credits, bought in advance. Prices are shown before you pay. Payment is handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

Credits do not expire while your account is open. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

Prints

Prints are made and shipped by a professional print house on our behalf, and we will tell you when your order is dispatched. If a print arrives damaged or misprinted, tell us and we will sort it out.

If something goes wrong

We would rather fix it than argue about it. If a crafted image did not come out as it should, tell us — depending on what happened we will craft it again at no cost, return your credits, or refund you. You do not have to prove anything.

Requests that look like a deliberate attempt to obtain crafts without paying for them are reviewed by a person before anything is refunded.

The service uses generative software and results vary. We cannot promise that a particular craft will please you, that a likeness will meet a particular standard, or that the service will be available without interruption.

We are a small studio. To the extent the law allows, our liability to you for anything arising out of these terms is limited to what you have paid us in the twelve months before the problem arose. Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Closing an account

You can close your account at any time by asking us. We will delete your collection and anything still held, except where we must keep something to resolve an open dispute or because the law requires it. We may close an account that breaks these terms, and we will say why.

Changes, and the law that applies

Privacy

Last updated 8 August 2026

We do not sell your information. We do not share your photographs. We do not train anything on your face. We keep your source photograph only as long as we are working with it, and then we delete it.

We do advertise, and like any business that advertises we use tags that recognise a browser. Those tags never see your photographs or your account — only that someone visited. The cookies section below says exactly what that means.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you make an account.
  • Your photographs, when you upload them.
  • Your crafted images, stored in your collection.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. We see that a payment succeeded and what it was for. We never see your card number.
  • Your shipping address, if you order a print. It goes to the print house so they can post it to you.
  • Basic technical information that any website receives — pages visited, browser, error reports.
  • Your messages to us, including conversations with the Concierge.

What we do with it

Your email address identifies your account and lets us send you what you asked for — sign-in links, receipts, dispatch notes.

Your photographs are used to craft your images and for nothing else. We do not train models on them, sell them, or share them with anyone except the services below that make the crafting and printing work. If we ever want to show a piece publicly we will ask first.

How long we keep things

Source photographs are deleted once the work using them is finished. If a dispute is open we keep the source and the images crafted from it until it is closed — a dispute may stay open up to a year, and we will tell you if yours is one we are holding.

Crafted images stay in your collection until you remove them or close your account. Account and purchase records are kept while your account is open, and afterwards as long as tax rules require.

Who else touches your data

A small number of established services run Liten & Co. Each does one job, receives only what that job needs, and may not use your data for anything else.

  • Vercel — hosting.
  • Supabase — the database and image storage.
  • Stripe — payments. Card details go to Stripe, never to us.
  • Replicate and Google — the crafting itself. Your photograph is processed and not retained for training.
  • Prodigi — print production and shipping. Receives your image and shipping address for that order.
  • Resend — sending email.
  • OpenAI — assessing photograph quality and finished crafts, and answering questions as the Concierge.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your photographs, crafted images or account contents with anyone for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what we hold, correct anything wrong, delete your account and everything in it, or send you a copy of your data. Write to us and we will do it — we will not make you justify the request and we will not charge for it.

The only thing we may hold back is something needed to resolve an open dispute, or that the law requires us to keep. If that applies we will say so, and say why.

If you are in California the CCPA gives you these rights formally, including the right not to be treated differently for exercising them. We do not sell personal information. Advertising tags may count as “sharing” under California law, so you can tell us to stop and we will, and your browser’s Global Privacy Control signal is honoured.

Children

Liten & Co is for adults, and you must be 18 or over to hold an account. A family photograph that includes children may be uploaded by an adult who has the right to use it. A photograph of a child on their own is not permitted and we refuse it.

Security

Images are held in private storage and reached only through links that expire. Payment details never reach us. Access to production data is limited to the people who need it to run the service.

No system is perfect. If something happens that affects your data we will tell you, rather than hope you do not notice.

Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies keep you signed in and remember your session. The site does not work without them.

Analytics and advertising cookies help us understand how people find Liten & Co and whether our advertising reaches the right people. That includes tags from advertising platforms, which can recognise a browser that has visited us before.

What those tags see is the fact of a visit and the pages viewed. They never see your photographs, your crafted images, or the contents of your account. You can refuse them through your browser or device settings, and the site will work exactly as before.

Where your data is, and changes to this page
