Hooks

HookCC responsibility
#entryGate[data-state]signed-out · link-sent · signed-in. Drive this attribute; the panels follow it.
#gateEmail · #gateSendExisting signInWithOtp via SigninModal.tsx. Wire to it, do not rebuild. Disable send until the address parses.
#gateCodeOptional, pre-auth. Hold, do not redeem — redemption requires an account. Apply on return from the link.
#gateMsg · #gateMsgTextOne line, never a banner. Unhide with the reason.
#gateSent · #gateSentAddrLink-sent panel and the address it went to.
#gateHeldCodeUnhide only when a code was entered pre-auth.
#gateResend · #gateChangeEmailResend; return to signed-out with the address kept.
#gateIdentity · #gateAvatarAddress and its first initial.
#gateBalanceBare number. The word "credits" is markup, not data.
#gateEnterInto the workshop. Native disabled at zero balance.
#gateCodeIn · #gateRedeemPost-auth redemption against access_codes. Idempotent per account.
#gateRedeemMsg · #gateRedeemTextSwap .ok / .warn / .err on the parent.

What this gate does not do

Consent is not here. It lives at upload as #s1consent — "I have the right to use this photo and agree to the Terms" — and that sentence is about a photograph. At the door there is no photograph. Moving it here would ask someone to affirm a right over an image they haven't chosen. It stays at intake.

Nothing is sold here. Buying credits is a considered act inside Account, never an interstitial at a door.
