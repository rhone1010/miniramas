## bronze (2,312 chars)

> A finished portrait sculpture — settled, unhurried, in the implied prior attention of a figure just turning back to meet the viewer.
>
> Shoulders turned slightly from camera; head returns to a three-quarter view, eyes meeting the viewer. Arms descend naturally; both hands resolved at plinth level — on the base, the plaque, or each other. Never terminating in unsupported space.
>
> Soft directional museum light; the rotated shoulder takes quiet shadow.
>
> Square 1:1 frame. Inscription plaque integrated at the base.
>
> Create a desirable collectible rather than a catalog photograph. Capture a candid moment using one subtle personality — warm, thoughtful, confident, reflective, curious, creative, or determined — expressed through natural posture, expression, and gesture. Vary between folded hands, pedestal interaction, chin support, relaxed forearms, slight torso turns, open shoulders, and implied movement. Avoid rigid symmetry, stiff posture, or direct passport-style presentation.
>
> Randomly select Collector, Monument, Gallery Walk-Up, Curator, or Studio Hero photography. Use 35–70mm lenses, favoring 35–50mm from closer distances. Vary eye-level, low-angle, elevated, three-quarter, and over-shoulder viewpoints. Sculpture fills 55–70% of frame while preserving accurate facial proportions.
>
> Place within refined galleries, ateliers, libraries, or collector-inspired interiors with natural daylight, atmospheric depth, and subtle volumetric light. Photograph as a featured piece in a premium design magazine. Favor editorial storytelling over showroom documentation. Preserve Liten visual identity through controlled variation.
>
> Portrait of a person rendered as 3D polished bronze sculpture — face and hair rendered in classic patinated bronze, with clothing optionally in muted patinated bronze, ceramic, enamel, or textile-like sculpted surface used subtly and tastefully, dignified not costume-like, on a round marble pedestal in a museum gallery, illuminated by a volumetric beam of natural light streaming from a skylight above, lit by soft directional studio lighting that brings out facial detail, with a small plaque on the base reading "Liten & Co · 2025". Likeness must be exact — the subject's face, expression, head angle and tilt, and gaze direction must match the source photograph precisely.

DRAMATIC FORESHORTENING: the reaching hand and arm thrust forward toward the viewer, appearing larger in the frame than the head behind them — conveying kinetic energy and the intent of active emergence. The page is visible behind and beside the figure, its edges and corners showing. The composition is INTENTIONALLY ASYMMETRIC — split into two states:
>
> ONE SIDE — fully three-dimensional graphite sculpture thrusting forward: the subject's head, far hand, far arm, and far shoulder are physical graphite sculpture with real sculptural mass, dimensional depth, and palpable forward motion.
