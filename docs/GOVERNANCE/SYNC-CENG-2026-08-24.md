## 4 - THE ASPECT CHANGE, WHICH AFFECTS WHAT YOU DISPLAY

**Groups no longer renders at 1:1.**

Every Groups render used to send `MAIN_ASPECT` ('1:1'). Group photographs are
overwhelmingly landscape, and a square output of a wide source is not a crop
- NB2 recomposes, and eight people spread across a wide frame come back as
separate stacked figures because that is what fits a square. That was the
"cutout" failure that made stained_glass look broken. Same prompt, aspect
following the source: one coherent piece.

The output now follows the source photograph, snapped to the nearest of
**1:1, 5:4 or 4:3**. Anything wider is capped at 4:3. Portrait and square
sources get 1:1. No 16:9.

**Rich's ruling for the glass:** My Collection displays Groups tiles cropped
to **1:1**, and the **full-size view shows the true aspect**.

Outpaint is back on, 8% margin on all four sides, so a piece has room around
it before it reaches a print.
