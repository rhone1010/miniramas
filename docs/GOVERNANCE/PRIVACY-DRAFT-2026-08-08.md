We do not sell your information. We do not share your photographs. We do not
train anything on your face. We keep your source photograph only as long as
we are working with it, and then we delete it.

## 2 · What we collect

**Your email address**, when you make an account.

**Your photographs**, when you upload them to have something crafted.

**Your crafted images**, which we store in your collection.

**Payment information** — handled entirely by Stripe. We see that a payment
succeeded and what it was for. We never see your card number and we do not
store it.

**Your shipping address**, if you order a print. It goes to the print house
so they can post it to you.

**Basic technical information** that any website receives: the pages you
visited, roughly when, what browser you used, and error reports when
something breaks.

**Your messages to us**, including conversations with the Concierge.

## 3 · What we do with it

Your email address identifies your account, and lets us send you what you
asked for — sign-in links, receipts, dispatch notes.

**Your photographs are used to craft your images, and for nothing else.** We
do not use them to train models. We do not sell them. We do not share them
with anyone except the processors listed below who make the crafting and the
printing work. We do not use them to advertise Liten & Co. If we ever want to
show a piece publicly we will ask you first.

Your shipping address goes to the print house for that order.

Technical information is used to keep the site working.

## 4 · How long we keep things

**Source photographs** are deleted once the work using them is finished — the
craft, or a gift commission through to acceptance.

**If a dispute is open, we keep the source photograph and the images crafted
from it until it is closed**, because we cannot look into a complaint without
them. A dispute may stay open up to a year. If yours is one that is keeping
your photograph with us, we will tell you.

**Crafted images** stay in your collection until you remove them or close
your account.

**Account and purchase records** are kept while your account is open, and
afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

**Messages** are kept while they may still be relevant to your account.

## 5 · Who else touches your data

We use a small number of established services to run Liten & Co. Each does
one job, receives only what that job needs, and is not permitted to use your
data for anything else.

- **Vercel** — hosting the site.
- **Supabase** — the database and the storage where images are kept.
- **Stripe** — payments. Card details go to Stripe and never to us.
- **Replicate** and **Google** — the crafting itself. Your photograph is sent
  to be processed and is not retained for training.
- **Prodigi** — print production and shipping. Receives your image and your
  shipping address for that order.
- **Resend** — sending email.
- **OpenAI** — assessing photograph quality and finished crafts.

## 6 · Your choices

You can ask us to:

- tell you what we hold about you
- correct anything wrong
- delete your account and everything in it
- send you a copy of your data

Write to `[PRIVACY EMAIL]` and we will do it. We will not make you justify
the request, and we will not charge for it.

The only thing we may hold back is something we need to resolve an open
dispute, or that the law requires us to keep. If that applies we will say so
and say why.

**If you are in California**, the CCPA gives you these rights formally,
including the right not to be treated differently for exercising them. We
would not, and there is nothing to opt out of, because we do not sell or
share personal information.

## 7 · Children

Liten & Co is for adults. You must be 18 or over to hold an account.

A family photograph that includes children may be uploaded by an adult who
has the right to use it. **A photograph of a child on their own is not
permitted**, and we refuse it.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn
that we have, we delete it.

## 8 · Security

Images are held in private storage and reached only through links that expire.
Payment details never reach us. Access to production data is limited to the
people who need it to run the service.

No system is perfect. If something happens that affects your data, we will
tell you rather than hope you do not notice.

## 9 · Where your data is

Liten & Co operates in the United States, and the services above process data
in the United States and elsewhere. Using the service means your data is
handled in those places.
