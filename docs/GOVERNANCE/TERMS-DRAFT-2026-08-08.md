## 2 · What the service does

You upload a photograph. We use it to craft a new image — a stylised
portrait in one of our finishes. You can keep that image, and you can order
it as a print.

The crafted image is generated by software. It is a new work made from your
photograph, not a filter applied to it, and it will not be identical to the
photograph you supplied.

## 3 · Your account

You need an account to buy anything. Keep your sign-in details to yourself;
anything done through your account is treated as done by you.

You must be 18 or over to hold an account and to make a purchase.

## 4 · The photographs you upload

**You must have the right to upload the photograph.** By uploading, you
confirm that you either took it or have permission to use it, and that every
person shown in it has agreed to it being used this way.

**Photographs of children.** A family photograph that includes children is
fine. A photograph of a child on their own is not, and we will refuse it.

**You must not upload** photographs of people who have not agreed to it,
images you do not have the right to use, sexual content, or anything
unlawful. We can refuse or remove anything, and close an account that does
this.

## 5 · What you are granting us

**A licence to craft your image, and nothing else.**

You keep ownership of your photograph. You grant us permission to store and
process it for the sole purpose of crafting the images you ask for, and to
produce and fulfil any print you order.

We do not use your photographs to train anything. We do not sell them, share
them, license them onward, or use them to advertise Liten & Co. If we ever
want to show a piece publicly, we will ask you first and you can say no.

This licence ends when we delete the photograph.

## 6 · How long we keep your photograph

**We delete your source photograph once the work using it is finished.**

We hold it only while it is needed: during a craft, and through a gift
commission until the piece is accepted.

**If a dispute is open, we keep it until the dispute is closed.** If you ask
for a refund or raise a complaint, we need your source photograph and the
images crafted from it to look at what happened — so we hold them while that
is unresolved. A dispute may stay open for up to a year, and we will tell you
if yours is one that is keeping your photograph with us. We would rather
close it quickly and will do what we can to reach you and put it right.

Your crafted images stay in your collection until you ask us to remove them,
or until your account is closed.

## 7 · What you own

Christmas card. You may use it for commercial purposes if you wish.

We keep our own materials — the finishes themselves, the prompts, the
software, the site, and everything on it that we made.

Requests that look like a deliberate attempt to obtain crafts without paying
for them are reviewed by a person before anything is refunded.
